About Ruma's Cake Arcade & Academy

Established in 2020, Ruma's Cake Arcade & Academy has become Matara's favorite destination for bespoke cakes, gourmet cupcakes, artisanal brownies, and professional baking education.

Whether you are planning a grand wedding, a memorable birthday, or aspiring to master the art of baking through our academy courses, we ensure every creation is baked with passion and decorated to perfection.

Classic Favorite

Decadent Chocolate Cake

Prep: 20 mins | Bake: 35 mins | Serves: 8-10

Ingredients

  • All-purpose flour
  • Sugar
  • Unsweetened cocoa powder
  • Baking powder & baking soda
  • Salt
  • Milk (buttermilk, almond, oat, or coconut)
  • Vegetable oil or coconut oil
  • Eggs (room temperature)
  • Vanilla extract & boiling water

Baking Instructions

1. Prep: Preheat oven to 350º F. Butter and flour two 9-inch cake pans. 2. Whisk Dry Ingredients: Add flour, sugar, cocoa, baking powder, baking soda, and salt into a bowl. Whisk thoroughly until well combined. 3. Mix Wet Ingredients: Add milk, oil, eggs, and vanilla. Mix on medium speed until combined. Carefully stir in boiling water until smooth. 4. Bake: Pour batter into pans. Bake for 30–35 minutes until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean. Cool completely before frosting.
Tea Time Special

Golden Butter Cake

Prep: 25 mins | Bake: 60 mins | Serves: 10-12

Ingredients

  • 1 cup (220 g) butter
  • 1 cup (220 g) caster sugar
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil
  • 5 large eggs
  • 1¾ cups (220 g) all-purpose flour
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • ½ cup (112 g) buttermilk
  • 1½ tsp vanilla extract

Baking Instructions

1. Prep: Have ingredients at room temperature. Preheat oven to 150°C (302°F). Line an 8-inch cake tin. Sift flour and baking powder together. 2. Cream Butter & Sugar: Cream butter, sugar, and oil for 5 minutes until pale and fluffy. Add eggs one at a time, beating well. 3. Combine Batter: Alternately fold in dry ingredients and buttermilk/vanilla mixture until just combined. Do not overmix. 4. Bake: Bake for 1 hour until golden. Cool in pan for 15 mins, then transfer to a wire rack.
Rich & Fudgy

Gourmet Fudge Brownies

Prep: 15 mins | Bake: 30 mins | Serves: 9 squares

Batter & Frosting

  • ½ cup butter & 1 cup white sugar
  • 2 eggs & 1 tsp vanilla extract
  • ⅓ cup cocoa powder & ½ cup flour
  • ¼ tsp salt & ¼ tsp baking powder
  • Frosting: 3 tbsp softened butter, 3 tbsp cocoa, 1 tbsp honey, 1 cup confectioners' sugar

Baking Instructions

1. Prep: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease an 8-inch square pan. 2. Batter: Melt 1/2 cup butter. Stir in sugar, eggs, and vanilla. Beat in cocoa, flour, salt, and baking powder. Spread into pan. 3. Bake: Bake 25 to 30 minutes until top is set and edges pull away slightly. 4. Frosting: Mix softened butter, cocoa, honey, vanilla, and powdered sugar until smooth. Spread over warm brownies.
Beautiful Swirl

Classic Marble Swirl Cake

Prep: 20 mins | Bake: 35 mins | Serves: 8-10

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup sugar & 1 cup milk
  • 2 large eggs & ½ cup softened butter
  • 2 tsp baking powder & 1 tsp vanilla
  • ½ tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp unsweetened cocoa powder

Baking Instructions

1. Prep: Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a 9-inch round pan. 2. Mix Batter: Beat flour, sugar, milk, eggs, butter, baking powder, vanilla, and salt until smooth (approx. 2 mins). 3. Create Marble: Reserve 3/4 cup batter and stir in cocoa. Pour white batter into pan, drop cocoa batter by spoonfuls on top, and swirl gently with a knife. 4. Bake: Bake for 30–35 minutes until toothpick inserted in center comes out clean.

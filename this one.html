Featured Recipes

Chocolate Cake

Whisk flour, cocoa, sugar, baking powder & soda. Add eggs, milk & oil. Carefully stir in boiling water and bake at 350°F for 30-35 mins.

Butter Cake

Cream butter, sugar, and oil. Add eggs one by one. Fold in flour & buttermilk. Bake at 150°C for 1 hour.

Brownies

Melt butter, stir in sugar, eggs, vanilla, cocoa & flour. Bake at 350°F for 25-30 mins. Top with honey cocoa frosting.

Marble Cake

Mix vanilla batter. Reserve 3/4 cup and mix with cocoa. Swirl cocoa batter into vanilla batter and bake for 30-35 mins.
